# Hydraulic Stamping Press – Technician Manual

Applies to the hydraulic stamping press with tonnage monitor, die cushion and central die lubrication.
All work described here is for trained maintenance technicians only.

## Low Hydraulic Pressure

**Symptoms**
- Press slows down or does not reach the bottom dead center; stroke aborts.
- Pressure gauge at the main valve block shows less than the setpoint.
- Oil drops or an oil film under hoses, fittings or the pump.
- Pump makes a whining or rattling noise.

**Related error codes:** E301 (Hydraulic pressure low)

**Step-by-step fix**
1. Stop the press with the slide at the top position and insert the slide safety block.
2. Apply lockout/tagout and release stored hydraulic pressure through the accumulator drain valve. Check the gauge reads 0 bar.
3. Check the oil level in the tank sight glass. Top up only with the specified hydraulic oil grade.
4. Inspect hoses and fittings for leaks, cracks and chafing. Replace damaged hoses; do not tape them.
5. Check the suction filter and the return filter indicators. Replace clogged filter elements.
6. Check the pressure relief valve setting against the hydraulic schematic.
7. If no leak is found, measure the pump delivery. A worn pump must be replaced by the hydraulic specialist.
8. Remove the safety block and lockout/tagout, start the pump, and check the pressure before running strokes.

**Safety note:** Apply lockout/tagout before any work and release all stored hydraulic pressure. Never search for leaks with your hand; a pinhole jet of hydraulic oil can penetrate the skin. Always use the slide safety block when working under the slide.

## Tonnage Monitor Deviation

**Symptoms**
- Tonnage monitor reports peak force above or below the learned envelope.
- Parts show cracks, wrinkles or incomplete forming.

**Related error codes:** E302 (Tonnage monitor deviation)

**Step-by-step fix**
1. Stop the press and check the current die number against the production order.
2. Compare the set die height (shut height) with the die setup sheet. Correct it if needed.
3. Measure the thickness of the coil or blank material at several points. Report variation outside tolerance to quality.
4. Apply lockout/tagout, insert the slide safety block, and inspect the die for slugs, broken punches or foreign objects.
5. Remove the safety block and lockout/tagout, run single strokes and watch the tonnage curve.
6. Do not re-learn the tonnage envelope unless the process engineer approves it.

**Safety note:** Apply lockout/tagout and use the slide safety block before reaching into the die area.

## Die Lubrication Low

**Symptoms**
- Lubrication reservoir level warning; dry or scratched part surfaces.
- Increased tonnage and galling marks on the die.

**Related error codes:** E303 (Die lubrication low)

**Step-by-step fix**
1. Check the reservoir level and refill with the specified forming lubricant.
2. Apply lockout/tagout before checking the spray nozzles in the die area.
3. Clean blocked spray nozzles and check the lubrication lines for kinks.
4. Remove lockout/tagout and run the lubrication pump manually to check every nozzle sprays.
5. Note the refill in the maintenance log so the refill interval can be adjusted.

**Safety note:** Apply lockout/tagout before any work in the die area. Forming lubricant makes the floor slippery; clean up spills immediately.
